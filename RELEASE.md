# Truth's GTA5 Story Mode+ — v1.0.0

First public release for GTA V Enhanced Story Mode. Earlier 2.x labels belonged
to private development builds; public versioning begins at **v1.0.0**.

## Highlights

- Green/black translucent boxed interface, animated binary background, complete
  header/footer logos, descriptions, category navigation and controller support.
- Startup notification now displays **Truths Story Mode+** on its own line, with
  `Loaded v1.0.0. Press F5 to open.` underneath.
- Fourteen categories with 224 top-level rows plus dynamic catalogs and landmarks.
- Player, cash, vehicle spawning/tuning, weapons, clothing, world, traffic,
  teleport, scenario and sandbox features. Full inventory: [FEATURES.md](FEATURES.md).
- Gameplay-camera tracking and on-foot first-person FOV are released features.
  The high-speed stop around 180 MPH is fixed; the optional MPH limiter remains
  configurable. These were confirmed in-game by the project owner.
- Installation and content paths: [README.md](README.md), [ADDONS.md](ADDONS.md).
- Source, local test/build tools, privacy preflight, checksums and source citations included.

## Download / install

Release asset generated by the packager:
`Truths_GTA5_Story_Mode_Plus_v1.0.0.zip`.

Exit GTA. Copy only the archive's `scripts` folder into the game installation.
Keep your working Enhanced loaders and existing saved menu data. Press F5 in
offline Story Mode. Do not install the tests/tools folders into the game's scripts.
